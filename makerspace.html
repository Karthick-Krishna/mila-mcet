FabLab

Makerspace & Rapid Prototyping

High-precision fabrication tools turning digital CAD designs into physical hardware prototypes in record time.

Industrial 3D Printing

High-precision SLA resin and dual-extruder FDM printers for functional enclosure prototyping and mechanical brackets.

  • Build Volume: Up to 350x350x400mm
  • Materials: PLA, ABS, PETG, TPU, Carbon Fiber

CO2 Laser Cutter & Engraver

100W CO2 precision laser system for rapid cutting of acrylic, wood, leather, delrin, and prototype chassis fabrication.

  • Bed Size: 900 x 600 mm
  • 0.05mm positioning precision

PCB Prototyping & SMD Lab

Rapid chemical-free CNC PCB isolation routing machine and temperature-controlled hot air SMD rework stations.

  • Double-sided PCB milling
  • Stereo Inspection Microscopes
