Accelerating technology readiness from lab-scale Proof-of-Concept to commercially compliant, manufacturing-ready product units.

Pre-Compliance Testing

Preliminary EMC/EMI testing, ingress protection (IP ratings) validation, voltage surge testing, and environmental thermal cycling to de-risk official regulatory certifications (BIS, CE, FCC).
