FLAGSHIP GRANT
EDII-TN • GOVT OF TAMIL NADU

Innovation Voucher Program (IVP)

Dr. MCET CIBIE is a recognized knowledge partner and facilitator for the Entrepreneurship Development and Innovation Institute (EDII) Tamil Nadu IVP scheme, unlocking commercialization capital for tech ventures.

IVP VOUCHER TIERS & TRACK RECORD:
  • Voucher A (Up to ₹2.00 Lakhs): For feasibility studies, technical design, patent search, and initial simulation via MCET C-DAT.
  • Voucher B (Up to ₹5.00 Lakhs): For developing advanced functional prototypes, field testing, and commercial pilot deployment.
  • MCET Impact: 3 AgriTech ventures (Coconut Dehusker, Tractor Rotavator, Automatic Jeevamirtham Machine) awarded ₹7.5 Lakhs cumulative grants!

CENTRAL GOVT
MINISTRY OF MSME • GOVT OF INDIA

MSME Innovative Scheme (DFO Coimbatore)

Conducted in direct collaboration with the MSME Development & Facilitation Office (DFO), Coimbatore. Provides financial assistance to nurture innovative ideas into marketable products.

SCHEME HIGHLIGHTS:
  • Idea Grant: Financial grant up to ₹15 Lakhs per approved idea for proof-of-concept development.
  • Design Grant: Up to ₹40 Lakhs for specialized manufacturing and industrial design interventions.
  • IP Support: Full reimbursement for Indian and international patent and trademark filings.

STARTUPTN • TANSIM

TNSI Grants & TANSEED Seed Fund

Supported through StartupTN Coimbatore Regional Hub. Dr. MCET student founders participate in Tamil Nadu Student Innovator (TNSI) awards and scale into TANSEED equity-free seed grants.

FUNDING HIGHLIGHTS:
  • TNSI Award: ₹1,00,000 cash grant for regional top student innovators.
  • TANSEED: Up to ₹15 Lakhs equity-free seed funding for qualified startup ventures.

Roadmap

Funding Application & Disbursement Roadmap

From concept submission to CIBIE evaluation, technical committee sanctioning, and milestone-based disbursement.

01

Concept Submission

Submit technical brief, problem statement, prototype design, and estimated budget via MILA portal.

02

Technical Review

CIBIE review committee evaluates feasibility, IP novelty, and market potential in Pollachi and beyond.

03

Committee Pitching

Demonstrate physical or digital MVP before the EDII-TN / MSME / CIBIE scrutiny panel.

04

Tranche Disbursement

Milestone-linked grants released directly into dedicated project account alongside lab facilities.
