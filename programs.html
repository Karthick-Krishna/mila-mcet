Structured Incubation Programs

Nurturing student makers, agricultural innovators, and engineering researchers into high-impact enterprises through MILA and EIC at Dr. MCET Pollachi.

MILA Student Pre-Incubation

Ideation to Working Prototype (PoC)

Open to Dr. MCET engineering students across all departments to develop raw ideas into viable minimum viable products (MVPs) with faculty mentorship.

  • AICTE IDEA Lab 24/7 fabrication access
  • Seed prototype grants up to ₹50,000
  • Free patent search & prior art support via EIC
  • Academic project to startup conversion credits
6 Months Duration

EIC Resident Physical Incubation

Resident Scaling & Commercialization

Full residency in the NPT Main Building Incubation Wing with private cabins, advanced machine tooling, and state grant backing.

  • EDII-TN IVP Voucher A & B (₹2L & ₹5L) grants
  • Air-conditioned office cabins with gigabit LAN
  • MCET C-DAT & Altair CAE simulation access
  • Investor matchmaking & StartupTN Coimbatore demo days
18 - 24 Months

Pollachi AgriTech & Rural Innovation

Farm Mechanization & Value-Addition

Capitalizing on Pollachi's agricultural heartland to build smart coconut de-huskers, multi-rotor rotavators, and automated organic fertilizer dispensers.

  • Direct test beds in Pollachi coconut groves & farms
  • EDII-TN IVP funding channel support
  • Heavy machining & welding workshop support
  • Mentorship from agricultural engineers & farmers
12 - 18 Months

Technology & Industrial Automation

Industry 4.0, IoT & Smart Robotics

Leveraging Dr. MCET's world-class Bosch and Mitsubishi Centre of Excellence to build automated robotic arms, IoT systems, and industrial AI controllers.

  • Mitsubishi PLC & SCADA industrial training benches
  • Bosch Rexroth hydraulics and pneumatics labs
  • MSME Innovative scheme grant assistance
  • Industrial pilot deployment across Coimbatore MSMEs
12 - 18 Months

Alumni & Women Innovators

Commercial Scale-Up & Market Access

Empowering Dr. MCET alumni and women entrepreneurs to scale technology ventures with strategic mentoring, angel networks, and MSME registrations.

  • Priority nomination for StartupTN TANSEED
  • Corporate legal, trademark & IPR drafting
  • Global Dr. MCET alumni mentor network access
  • Cloud architecture & SaaS incubation support
Flexible / 12 Months
