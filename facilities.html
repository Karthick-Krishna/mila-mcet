Centres of Excellence & Advanced Labs

Empowering innovators at Dr. MCET Pollachi with an AICTE IDEA Lab, C-DAT design & testing suite, Altair CAE simulation centre, and Bosch/Mitsubishi industrial automation testbeds.

AICTE Sanctioned

AICTE IDEA Lab (Prototyping & Fabrication)

National-level multidisciplinary makerspace empowering Dr. MCET students and regional MSMEs with 24/7 rapid prototyping, 3D printing, laser machining, and embedded testing.

MACHINERY & TOOLS:
FDM & SLA Precision 3D Printers High-Power CO2 Laser Cutter CNC Router & Milling Unit Automated PCB Prototyping Machine Vinyl Cutter & Welding Station
Design & Analysis

MCET – C-DAT (Design, Analysis & Testing)

Premier centre specializing in finite element analysis (FEA), computational fluid dynamics (CFD), structural failure analysis, and physical strain testing for machinery and automotive parts.

CAPABILITIES & SUITES:
ANSYS FEA / CFD Multi-physics SolidWorks Premium CAD Suite Dynamic Universal Testing Machine Digital Strain Gauge Indicator Torsional Fatigue Tester
CAE Simulation

MCET – ALTAIR Centre of Excellence

Industrial CAE facility dedicated to generative design, topology optimization, EV composite body framing, and crashworthiness analysis for advanced hardware incubatees.

SOFTWARE & BENCHES:
Altair HyperWorks Simulation Altair OptiStruct Optimization Radioss Crash Simulation EV Powertrain Simulation Units High-Performance Compute Server
Industry 4.0

Mitsubishi & Bosch Automation Centre

Industry-grade automation laboratory with programmable logic controllers (PLCs), SCADA networks, Rexroth electro-hydraulics, and industrial servo motor control test stations.

MACHINERY & TESTBENCHES:
Mitsubishi iQ-F / iQ-R PLCs Bosch Rexroth Pneumatic Benches Industrial Servo Motor Drives SCADA & IoT Telemetry Panels Vision Inspection Camera Units
