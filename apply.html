Why Incubate at MILA?

MILA and the Entrepreneurship & Incubation Centre (EIC) offer an end-to-end incubation ecosystem tailored for student innovators, alumni founders, faculty researchers, and regional entrepreneurs.

  • Advanced Prototyping Infrastructure:

    Direct access to AICTE IDEA Lab with 3D printers, laser cutters, CNC routing, and PCB fabrication tools, plus C-DAT and Altair simulation software.

  • Grant Funding & Seed Support:

    Assistance with EDII-TN Innovation Voucher Program (IVP-A up to ₹2L, IVP-B up to ₹5L), MSME Idea Hackathon grants (up to ₹15L), and StartupTN schemes.

  • Dedicated Mentorship & IP Support:

    One-on-one guidance from seasoned industry mentors, patent attorneys for IPR and trademark filing, and business model validation.

  • Resident Space & Ecosystem Access:

    Furnished startup cabins, co-working hot desks, high-speed internet, conference facilities, and official commercial business address at MILA Campus.

Incubation Admission Journey

A transparent, merit-driven evaluation process to support ventures from concept to commercial viability.

Stage 01

Online Application

Submit your founder background and startup proposal via the official application form for preliminary review.

Stage 02

Pitch & Scrutiny Panel

Present your working prototype and business idea before the MILA Technical Advisory Committee.

Stage 03

Onboarding & Lab Access

Formal incubation agreement, allotment of cabin / hot desk, IDEA Lab pass, and seed grant orientation.
