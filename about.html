About MILA & CIBIE

MCET Incubation and Innovation Launchpad Association (MILA Club) and the Centre for Innovation, Business Incubation and Entrepreneurship (CIBIE) at Dr. Mahalingam College of Engineering and Technology, Pollachi.

OUR VISION

Inculcating Innovation-Driven Entrepreneurship

"Established in the year 2015, CIBIE and MILA are committed to nurturing technical minds into enterprise creators, empowering engineering students, alumni, and regional grassroots innovators to build commercially viable, technology-driven startup companies solving critical agricultural and industrial challenges."

Dr. MCET Institutional Mandate
CORE OBJECTIVES

Infrastructure, Validation & Scaling

  • Common Incubation Facilities: Providing plug-and-play Work Space, Conference Rooms, IT infrastructure, 24/7 power backup, and library support.
  • Idea Validation & Mentoring: Refining concepts through proof-of-concept testing and sharing abstracts with TNSI, Forge Accelerator, and StartupTN.
  • Government Seed Grants: Guiding startups to win EDII-TN Innovation Voucher Program (IVP) funding (₹2L - ₹5L) and Ministry of MSME schemes.
