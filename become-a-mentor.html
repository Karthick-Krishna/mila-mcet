Why Mentor at MILA?

Dr. MCET MILA hosts over 40+ dynamic student and faculty ventures solving problems in Electric Vehicles, IoT automation, Agritech, CleanTech, and AI. As a mentor, you can:

  • Early Access to High-Potential Deals: First look at investment opportunities in deeply vetted hardware and software startups.
  • Lead Exclusive Masterclasses: Share domain thought-leadership in our Mentor Cafe and executive bootcamps.
  • Institutional Recognition: Official empanelment on the MCET Incubation Advisory Board.
