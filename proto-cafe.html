Proto Cafe (Weekly Prototyping Clinic)

Weekly Friday open studio where founders sit down with senior fabrication engineers to troubleshoot, slice CAD, and rapid prototype.

Every Friday • 3:00 PM to 7:00 PM

Walk in with rough napkin sketches, broken 3D models, or malfunctioning circuit schematics. Our in-house technical team helps you test, debug, solder, and print on the spot.

  • Free 3D filament testing quota
  • Component library access
  • Oscilloscope & power supply stations
