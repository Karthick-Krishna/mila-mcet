MILA – Virtual Incubation Programme

Virtual Incubation Programme

MCET Incubation and Innovation Launchpad Association (MILA) offers a virtual incubation programme designed to support aspiring entrepreneurs, student innovators, and early-stage startups in transforming innovative ideas into market-ready solutions. The programme provides structured guidance, expert mentorship, and access to entrepreneurial resources to help startups validate their business models and build sustainable ventures.

Startup-Focused Incubation Journey

Through a startup-focused incubation journey, participants receive support in business model development, product validation, market research, go-to-market planning, financial planning, and growth strategies. The programme also facilitates connections with industry experts, mentors, investors, and ecosystem partners to strengthen startup capabilities and explore business opportunities.

Market Readiness & Investor Engagement

MILA aims to create an enabling environment for entrepreneurs to overcome early-stage challenges, refine their products and services, and develop scalable business models. With access to mentoring, networking opportunities, funding guidance, and relevant institutional resources, startups can strengthen their readiness for market entry, pilot implementation, and investor engagement.

The Virtual Incubation Programme extends MILA's startup support beyond physical workspace, enabling entrepreneurs to access incubation guidance and ecosystem opportunities while developing innovative solutions across diverse sectors.

Eligibility Criteria

Requirements and prerequisites for prospective startups, student innovators, and founders.

01

Startup Idea / Product Readiness

Startups should have a validated business idea, proof of concept, functional prototype, or Minimum Viable Product (MVP). Preference may be given to ventures that have progressed towards product development and market validation.

02

Startup Location

Startups and entrepreneurial ventures based in India are eligible to apply, subject to the programme's requirements.

03

Stage of Startup

Student entrepreneurs, aspiring founders, early-stage startups, and emerging ventures seeking structured incubation support are encouraged to apply.

04

Innovation and Problem-Solving

The proposed venture should demonstrate innovation, address a real-world problem, and offer a meaningful solution with potential for practical implementation.

05

Founder Commitment

At least one founder or designated team member should be committed to participating in incubation activities, mentorship sessions, progress reviews, and programme-related engagements.

06

Scalability and Sustainability

The startup should demonstrate potential for market adoption, business growth, and long-term sustainability through a viable business model.

07

Alignment with MILA's Focus Areas

Startups working in areas such as Embedded Systems, Internet of Things (IoT), Industrial Automation, and Artificial Intelligence (AI), along with other innovative sectors, are encouraged to apply.
